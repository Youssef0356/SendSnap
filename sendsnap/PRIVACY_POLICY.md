# Privacy Policy for SendSnap

**Last Updated: April 14, 2026**

Welcome to **SendSnap - Fast WiFi File Transfer**. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "Service").

---

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

### Personal Information
SendSnap does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to our servers.

### File Transfers
All file transfers performed through SendSnap occur directly between devices over your local WiFi network (Peer-to-Peer). We do **not** upload your files to any cloud server or third-party service. Your data stays entirely within your local environment.

### Local Discovery
The app uses mDNS (multicast DNS) to discover other devices on the same network. This discovery process is local and does not involve any external tracking.

---

## 2. Permissions Required

To provide our service, SendSnap requires the following permissions on your device:

*   **Storage / Photos & Videos**: Necessary to select files to send and to save received files to your device.
*   **WiFi / Network State**: Required to establish a connection between devices for file transfer.
*   **Nearby Devices / Location**: (Android 12+ / iOS) Required by the system to scan for and connect to other devices on the same local network. We do **not** track your physical location.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

We use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements in the app. These ads help us keep the service free for everyone.

*   **AdMob Data**: Google AdMob may collect and use certain data, including your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and technical information about your device, to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
*   **Privacy Links**: For more information on how Google uses your data, please visit [Google’s Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

---

## 5. Security
We value your trust in using our Peer-to-Peer transfer service. Since transfers are local to your WiFi network, they are as secure as the network itself. We recommend using a password-protected WiFi network for maximum security.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
